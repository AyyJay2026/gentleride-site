GENTLE RIDE

Smooth braking, happy riders

Gentle Ride is a driving coach for people who carry passengers. It gives you a gentle, real-time cue when your driving gets abrupt, so you can ease off and give everyone a smoother, more comfortable ride.

Put your phone on the charging pad or a firm mount, tap Start and drive. That's it.

How it helps

  • A soft "radar" tick: silent while you drive smoothly, then ticking faster and higher as braking or acceleration gets more abrupt. Ease off until it goes quiet.
  • Prefer something else? Choose a voice, a soft beep, chime or ukulele strum, plus an optional screen flash.
  • Alerts mix with your music and navigation.

What it notices

  • Abrupt braking
  • Lift-off jolts: the sudden slowing when you come off the pedal too quickly in an electric car with strong regenerative braking (one-pedal driving)
  • Jerky changes in speed
  • Heavy acceleration (optional)
  • On/off pedal pumping: speeding up and slowing down over and over

Track your progress

  • A smoothness score from 0 to 100 for every trip
  • A timeline of each trip's events, and your trip history
  • Relaxed, Balanced and Strict settings, to match how sensitive your passengers are

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Your trips stay on your phone.
  • GPS is used only for speed, never your location.

Safety first

Gentle Ride never alerts during very hard, emergency-style braking. Always brake as hard as the road needs. Set it up before you drive, and never text or use your phone while driving. Alerts are sounds, so there's no need to look at the screen.

Gentle Ride isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose, prevent or treat any condition.
